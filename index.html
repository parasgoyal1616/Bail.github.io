BioArtificial Immune Liver (BAIL)

A Smart Implantable Platform for Real-Time Hepatitis B Detection, Immune Activation, and Liver Regeneration
Presenters: Paras Goyal, Vansh Kamboj
B. Pharmacy 2nd Year

🔬 Project Abstract

Chronic Hepatitis B virus (HBV) infection remains a major global health challenge affecting approximately 296 million people worldwide and is a leading cause of liver cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma. Although current antiviral therapies effectively suppress viral replication, they fail to eradicate covalently closed circular DNA (cccDNA), resulting in persistent infection, immune exhaustion, lifelong treatment, and disease recurrence. To address these limitations, we propose the BioArtificial Immune Liver (BAIL), a conceptual smart implantable bioengineered platform that integrates regenerative medicine, immunotherapy, biosensing, and intelligent therapeutic control into a unified system.

BAIL is designed as a three-layer architecture comprising a Living Hepatic Tissue Module for continuous HBV biomarker detection and liver support, an Immune Surveillance and Activation Module incorporating engineered cells for targeted antiviral immunity, and a Smart Therapeutic Control Module integrating biosensors and AI-assisted decision-making for localized therapeutic intervention.

📐 BAIL Architecture: Three-Layer Smart System

Layer 1: Living Hepatic Tissue

Functional tissue for continuous viral detection and liver metabolic support using human iPSC-derived hepatocytes within a 3D biocompatible scaffold.

Layer 2: Immune Surveillance

Engineered immune microenvironment using Kupffer cells, NK cells, and cytotoxic T cells for precise targeted viral elimination.

Layer 3: Smart Therapeutic Control

Intelligent biosensing, automated AI analytics engines, and microfluidic channels for localized, controlled therapeutic release.
